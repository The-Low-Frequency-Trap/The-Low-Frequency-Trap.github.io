The finding

A simple question exposes a fundamental failure mode.

Frontier video–language models can describe a scene, yet often lose track of repeated events when the evidence unfolds over time. We call this reliability boundary the Low-Frequency Trap.

Abstract

Temporal understanding needs more than a final answer.

Video–language models are increasingly evaluated on broad video understanding benchmarks, but success on a final answer can conceal failures in the temporal reasoning that produced it. EventLapse measures whether models can maintain an accurate account of repeated events across a video. Our results reveal a sharp capability boundary: as event load grows, models frequently fail at elementary event bookkeeping—even when the task remains visually simple.
